Questions

Do I have to pay?

No. StudyPlan runs on a free Google Gemini key, so there is nothing to subscribe to.

Why does it need an API key?

The key lets the app ask Google's AI to write your notes, flashcards and quiz questions. You create it free at aistudio.google.com/apikey in about a minute and paste it into Settings. It stays on your device.

Is my study material private?

Your exams are stored in your account, which only you can open, so they follow you between devices. Your API key stays on your device. Only the text for the day being prepared is sent to Google's AI to write that day's material.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, in any phone browser. An Android app is planned.
